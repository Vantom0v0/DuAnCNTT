## Method
## Foundation of RAG
### Definition and key concepts
- Chunking, embedding and reranking: A typical RAG pipeline

### Techical components of RAG
- Two primary components: retriever and generator
- Modern RAG typically uses dense retrievers like Dense Passage
Retrieval (DPR). A bi-encode encode both the query and the documents
then calculate the similarity score. (e.g. FAISS).
- Generator used seq2seq models but most RAG uses an LLM now.

## Year-by-year progress in RAG
- 2017-2019: early QA piplines and dense retrieval models
- 2020: Introduction of RAG, DPR, REALM, FiD and MARGE
- 2021: Fusion-in-decoder, EMDR2, KILT benchmark, REDCODER
- 2022: RETRO, ATLAS, BlenderBOT3, Contriever
- 2023: LLM-RAG, Self-RAG, SURGE, Adaptive retrieval.
- 2024: SafeRAG, Multimodal RAG
- 2025: GraphRAG, Agentic RAG, mtRAG benchmark

### 2023
- Knowledge-grounded dialogue: dialogue systems to improve consistency
and informativeness. Kumari et al.[47] incorporate retrieved persona 
and context snippets in long conversation modeling, showing that adding 
relevant knowledge improves response quality. Similarly, Kang et al.
[43] propose SURGE, which retrieves relevant subgraphs from a 
knowledge graph and uses them to bias the response generation. SURGE 
produces more coherent, factual responses grounded in the retrieved
knowledge.
- Benchmarks and evaluation: Knowledge-intensive benchmarks track RAG 
progress. OpenQA tasks (Natural Questions, TriviaQA, HotpotQA) and the
KILT benchmark suite (including QA, fact-checking, slot filling, etc.)
are standard evaluation sets.
- Evaluation tools: RAGAS, RAGTruth corpus

### 2025
- Graph with text retrieval
- Agentic and selective retrieval: SIM-RAG, Self-Routing RAG

## Evaluation of RAG systems
### Evaluation Dimensions and Metrics
- Retrieval accuracy: Recal@k, Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR), Mean 
Average Precision (MAP)
- Generation quality: Exact Match (EM), F1 Score, BLEU, ROUGE, 
Faithfulness/Hallucination rate.

### Benchmarking and Datasets
- Natural Questions (NQ), TriviaQA, and WebQuestions for open-domain
QA.
- FEVER and AveriTeC for fact-checking
- KILT benchmark suite
- BEIR evaluates retrieval

### RAGAS system
- A framework that evaluates factual consistency and grounding.

## Challenges
### Retrieval quality and relevance
- Modern neural retrievers with dense embeddings improve performance 
but still face issues like vocabulary mismatches, ambiguous queries, 
and domain-specific terminology.
- Determining the optimal number of retrieved passages (k) is also 
complex.

### Legal Domain
#### Medical Domain
- These applications commonly integrate authoritative medical 
databases such as PubMed and UpToDate to provide reliable, 
evidence-based responses.
- The data must be from rigorously vetted sources.
- Rapid Evolution of Medical Knowledge.
- Current RAG implementations perform best on factual inquiries while
open-ended diagnostic queries involving symptom analysis remain 
challenging and risk inappropriate advice.
- Bias in medical data
- RAG systems providing medical advice may fall under regulatory 
oversight such as FDA guidelines

## Discussion and future direction
### Future direction
- Multi-hop retrieval
- Multimodal and Agentic
- Structured knowledge intergration
- Real-time RAG

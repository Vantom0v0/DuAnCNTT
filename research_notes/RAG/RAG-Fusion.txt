## RAG-Fusion
- Reciprocal rank fusion is an algorithm commonly used in search to 
assign scores to every document and rerank them according to the
scores.
- Receive the original query, then use LLM to generate several new 
search queries based on the original. Each query performs vector seach 
for relevant documents but instead of sending those to the LLM, the 
model performs reciprocal rank fusion. Calculate the rrf score for 
each document before accumulating them. Documents are then fused
together and reranked according to their scores.

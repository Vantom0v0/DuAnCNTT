## Introduction
- Two categories of hallucination in NLG tasks:
+ Instrinsic: the output contradicts the source
+ Extrinsic: the output cannot be verified from the source.
- This survey categories hallucination into 2 types:
+ Factually hallucination: simply not true.
+ Faithfulness hallucination: is not faithful to the instruction.
- Main contributors: data, training, and inference stages.
- Even with the potential, RAG still suffers from hallucination.

## Hallucination causes
### From inference
- Over-confidence: 
+ Prioritizing fluency at the expense of faithfully 
adhering to the source context.
+ LLMs often exhibit a localized focus within their attention 
mechanisms, giving priority to nearby words and resulting in a notable 
deficit in context attention which is amplified in lenghty and 
comprehensive responses.
- Reasoning failure:
+ LLMs struggle to produce accurate answers in milti-hop QA if 
multiple associations exist between questions, due to reasoning
limitation
+ Can aswer "A is B" but fail when asked "B is A"

## Hallucination Mitigation
### RAG
- One-time retrieval (normal RAG, KGs, and parametric knowledge 
guiding, etc.)
- Iterative retrieval: adress multi-step reasoning and long form
answering (iterative retrieval, cot prompting, self-ask, active 
retrieval)
- Post hoc retrieval.

# 2. Overview
## 2.1. Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in 
healthcare: A systematic review.
- Datasets: mostly english with some chinese datasets.
- Naive RAG, Advanced RAG and Modular RAG are included.
- Proprietary models like GPT are the most used.
- Provide a systematic review of RAG-based methodologies applied in 
the medical domain.
- Provide an overview of evaluation methods, including metrics used to 
evaluate the performance of RAG pipelines.
- Disscuss about the dataset such as QA/Dialogue -> Compare models and
techniques -> Assess the proposed evaluation frameworks.

## 2.2. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) in Healthcare: A 
Comprehensive Review.
- Synthesize the existing architectural variants.
- Identify persistnet challenges.
- Evaluate using both standard metrics and clinical-specific metrics

## 2.3. Enhancing medical AI with retrieval-augmented generation: A 
mini narrative review.
- Similar to the other two papers

## 2.4. RAG in Health Care: A Novel Framework for Improving 
Communication and Decision-Making by Addressing LLM Limitations.
- also similar

## 2.5. Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine
- Just combining different datasets, retrievers and techniques.

## 2.6. Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language 
Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Proposing techniques and architectures to enchance the effectiveness
of GraphRAG in medical field.
- The approach is validated with various benchmarks and human 
clinicians.
- This method is build on top of GraphRAG with a new graph 
construction method bc GraphRAG is not enough.

# Notes
## Abstract
- A framework that enhances an LM’s quality and factuality through 
retrieval and self-reflection.
- Train a single LM to retrieve on-demand, generate and reflect on
retrieved passages and its own response using reflection tokens.
- Self-RAG improves LLM's generation quality

## Method
### Process
#### Step 1: Retrieve on Demand
1. From query x, it first decides whether it is necessary to retrieve
more documents by predicting the retrieval token.
2. The model predicts a token called "Retrieve" which has:
+ Yes: Do retrieval.
+ No: Retrieval isnt needed.
+ Continue to use evidence: Continue writing based on previously 
retrieved documents without triggering retriever.
3. If the retrieve token is "Yes" (or if its above a threshold), call
retriever to fetch k relevant documents

#### Step 2: Parallel Generation
- Process the retrieved documents in parallel rather than a single
block of text. For each document:
1. Relevance check (ISREL): LLM predicts a critique token of either 
Relevant or Irrelevant.
2. Drafting the segment: LLM drafts a continuation candidate segment
based on user's query, previous context, and the specific document.
Does this for each document, and we have multiple drafts for the same
user's query.

#### Step 3
- For every draft segment, LLMs generates critique tokens to grade
its own responses:
+ Is it supported? (ISSUP) -> Fully supported, Partially, or No 
Support.
+ Is it useful? (ISUSE): rank from 1 to 5.

#### Step 4
- Decide which response is the best to append to the final response:
- Scoring: runs a segmentation-level beam search to calculate:
Score = Probability of response p(y_t|x, d, y_<t) + S(critique)
S(critique) = w_ISREL.S_ISREL + w_ISSUP.S_ISSUP + w_ISUSE.S_ISUSE
Relevance Score (S_ISREL): The normalized probability of the Relevant 
token.
Support Score (S_ISSUP): This score is specially weighted to give 
partial credit for partial support.
- Customizable weights so that it can prioritize different criteria 
based on the dataset or task.

#### Step 5
1. Append the response with the highest score along with its 
self-reflection evaluations.
2. Repeat: moves to next sentence y_t+1, starting at Step 1. Continue
until the full answer is complete.

## Experiments
### Tasks and datasets
- Closed-dataset tasks: PubHealth, ARC-Challenge.
- Short-form generation tasks: open-domain QA like PopQA, 
TriviaQA-unfiltered.
- Long-form generation tasks: ALCE-ASQA, FactScore, MAUVE

## Ablation
- SELF-RAG demonstrates that naively trusting one retrieved passage is 
problematic.

# Notes
## Abstract
- Iterative RAG is good for multi-hop but it increases latency, cost,
and risk of introducing distracting evidence. -> needs a stopping 
strategy.
- Existing methods: predetermined iterations or rely on confidence
proxies that poorly relex whether an iteration is needed.

## Related work
- Iterative RAG
- Adaptive RAG:
+ Prompting-based
+ Confidence-based
+ Training-involved

## Stop-RAG
- Value-based approach to decide whether further retrieval is necessary 
in an iterative RAG pipeline

### Iterative RAG as an MDP
- The past interaction records become a state.
- At t-th iteration, the state includes original query and answer AND
the question, document and answer at the current iteration k.
- Two action: stop or cont
- If it reaches Stop then it transition into terminal state TERM.
- Reaches the maximum iteration also turns it into TERM.
- Rewards are assigned at terminal states and reflect the quality of
the answer generation at that state.

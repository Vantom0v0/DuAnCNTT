# Notes
## Abstract
- ReFeed generates inital outputs -> use retrieval to get relevant 
information -> incorporate the retrieved information into the 
in-context demonstration for output refinement.

## Method
- Prompt a model to generate an initial response to a query. Retrieve 
documents using the query and the initial response. Refine the 
response.
- Introduce 2 modules:
+ Diversity the initial generation step, producing multiple output
candidates. -> identify most reliable answer by exammining the broad
range of retrieved documents.
- Employs an ensemble approach that combines language model outputs 
from both before and after the retrieval feedback process then 
evaluate them.

# Notes
## Abstract
- MAIN-RAG (Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is 
a training-free RAG framework designed to collaboratively filter and 
score retrieved documents.
- It addresses a major vulnerability in standard RAG pipelines: the 
tendency of LLMs to ingest noisy or irrelevant retrieved documents, 
which degrades final answer quality and wastes context window space.
- Instead of retraining or fine-tuning models, MAIN-RAG uses 
cooperative prompting across multiple LLM agents and a dynamic, 
statistically-driven filter to clean the document pool before 
generating the final answer.

## Method
### The three agents
The framework splits the retrieval, filtering, and answering process 
among three specialized LLM agents:
1. Agent-1 (Predictor):
- Task: After initial retrieval, Agent-1 takes each retrieved document 
individually and drafts a candidate answer to the user's query based 
only on that document.
- Output: This creates a series of Document-Query-Answer (Doc-Q-A) 
Triplets.
2. Agent-2 (Judge):
- Task: Agent-2 evaluates whether the document actually provides 
relevant information to answer the query. It treats this evaluation as 
a True-or-False question, answering "Yes" or "No" with evidence.
- Relevance Quantification: To convert these text judgments into 
sortable scores, Agent-2 calculates the difference between the log 
probabilities of generating the "Yes" and "No" tokens (log odds): 
Score=logP("Yes")−logP("No"). This mathematical score serves as the 
sole criterion for document sorting and filtering.
3. Agent-3 (Final-Predictor):
- Task: Once Agent-2 scores, filters, and sorts the remaining 
documents, Agent-3 receives the clean, ordered document list and 
writes the final, correct response to the user

### The Adaptive Judge Bar
- Rather than relying on a static, arbitrary similarity threshold, it 
uses a self-calibrating bar based on score distributions:
+ The authors observed that LLMs assign confidently high, 
tightly-grouped scores to truly related documents. Conversely, noisy 
documents get lower, more widely scattered scores.
+ the baseline threshold starts at the average relevance score of the 
retrieved pool, the final threshold is adjusted using standard 
deviation (σ): τ_q−n⋅σ (n is a configurable hyperparameter)

### Document Ordering
- After filtering, the surviving documents are sorted in descending 
order (highest scoring first) 

## Experiments
### Tasks and Datasets
- Closed-set task: ARC-Challenge, MCQ from scientific exams.
- Open-Domain QA tasks: TriviaQA-unfiltered, and PopQA.
- Long-form generation tasks: ALCE-ASQA, MAUVE
### Baselines
- Without retrievals: Llama2_7B-13B, Llama3_8B and Mistral7B, 
instruction-tuned models like Alpaca7B,13B.
- Wtih retrieval:  evaluate models that incorporate retrieval, either 
during inference or throughout the training process. Self-RAG, a 
variant of Llama27B, Llama2-FT_7B, Ret-Llama2-chat_13B.
### Settings
-  Instantiate all three agents by pre-trained Mistral7B and Llama38B
without further tuning.
-  Employ the pre-trained Contriever-MS MARCO as the default retriever 
model
